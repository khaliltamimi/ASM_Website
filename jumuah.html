Jumu'ah Prayers

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِذَا نُودِيَ لِلصَّلَاةِ مِن يَوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ فَاسْعَوْا إِلَىٰ ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ وَذَرُوا الْبَيْعَ ۚ ذَٰلِكُمْ خَيْرٌ لَّكُمْ إِن كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ

O you who have believed, when [the adhan] is called for the prayer on the day of Jumu'ah (Friday), then proceed to the remembrance of Allah and leave trade. That is better for you, if you only knew. (Quran 62:9)

Sunnahs of Friday

  • Ghusl: Performing the ritual bath before heading to the prayer.
  • Clean Clothes: Wearing your best and cleanest clothes.
  • Perfume (Attar): Applying perfume (for men).
  • Siwak: Using the miswak to clean your teeth.
  • Surah Al-Kahf: Reciting Surah Al-Kahf during the day or the night before.
  • Durood: Sending abundant blessings upon the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).
  • Dua: Making earnest supplications, especially during the 'hour of acceptance'.
