Campus & Community Resources

Everything you need to thrive as a Muslim student in Milan.

Halal Food Near Campus

  • Kebab Bovisa: Great spot for a quick lunch between classes. (5 min walk)
  • Ristorante Al-Basha: Authentic Middle Eastern cuisine. (10 min by tram)
  • Macelleria Islamica: Local Halal butcher and grocery store.

Local Masajid

  • Centro Islamico di Milano: The main mosque in the city. (Friday prayers at 1:30 PM)
  • Masjid Al-Rahma: Smaller, community-focused mosque closer to the dorms.

Campus Prayer Space Guide

The university provides a dedicated multi-faith prayer room for students.

Location: Student Union Building, Room 302
Access: Open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Swipe your student ID card to enter.
Wudu Facilities: Available in the restrooms immediately adjacent to Room 302.

Please help us keep the prayer space clean and quiet. Do not leave personal items overnight.
