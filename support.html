Mentorship Program

Navigating university life can be challenging. Our Mentorship Program pairs incoming and current students with experienced upperclassmen to provide guidance, support, and friendship.

  • Academic Guidance: Get advice on picking classes and professors.
  • Spiritual Support: Connect with someone to help you maintain your faith on campus.
  • Social Connection: Make a friend who knows the ropes.
