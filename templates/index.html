About Us

Our Mission

The Muslim Student Association (ASM) strives to facilitate a welcoming and active community for Muslim students on campus. We aim to support our members in balancing their academic pursuits with their spiritual growth, while fostering an inclusive environment that promotes understanding and dialogue.

What We Do

  • Spiritual: Weekly Halaqas, daily prayers, and Jummah services.
  • Social: Brotherhood/Sisterhood events, dinners, and game nights.
  • Service: Community service projects and charity fundraisers.
  • Education: Islam Awareness Week, guest speakers, and workshops.
